WHAT A CARTRIDGE IS: not text, not weights. Internal tensors captured at a transformer boundary: the CUT3 slice - hidden state H3 plus K/V of layers 0-3 - forged by a two-pass forward over a source body, serialized to SSD with a SHA-256 file hash and tensor-level seals, verified on reload, injected at the layer-3 boundary while the frozen model recomputes layers 4-79. READ: append a question to the rebuilt memory and the model answers with the body text NOT in the prompt (state-without-text).
